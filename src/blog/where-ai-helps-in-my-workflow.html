I use AI tools every week, mostly in development. They've made me faster at the parts of a project that were never the interesting parts. I keep them away from the decisions that make a project good.

Where It Helps

  • Turning a finished component into clean, accessible markup.
  • Writing the boring parts of a build script or a form handler.
  • Checking a page for missing alt text, heading order and contrast.
  • Producing ten rough versions of a sentence so I can see what I don't want.

Where I Switch It Off

Structure, hierarchy and the first version of the copy. These are where the client's business shows up, and a model has no idea what that business is. Generated layouts look plausible and say nothing in particular.

What Clients Get from It

Mostly time. The hours saved on routine code go into testing on real devices and into the details people notice without knowing why.
