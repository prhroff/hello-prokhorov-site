(06)Questions

(05)
Do you design single landing pages?

Yes. A landing page goes through the same steps as a website — goals and audience, structure, then the visual design for desktop and mobile — just on one page.

Can you build it too?

Yes. I build the websites I design — in code or in Framer, whichever fits — so the design you approve is the site that goes live. That is Design & DevelopmentDesign & Development, from the start or after the design is done.

We already have a website. Do we have to start over?

Not necessarily. If the site mostly works, I can start by reviewing its usability and structure and give you a prioritised list of changes. If it needs replacing, the redesign starts from the brand and content you already have.

What does my developer get?

A Figma file with every page for desktop, tablet and mobile, built on components: colours, type and spacing as styles, buttons and forms with all their states. Plus a clickable prototype of the main flows, images and icons ready to export, and notes on motion and behaviour. I walk your developer through it on a call and stay on hand while they build.

How does a project start?

With a short message: what you’re building, whether there’s a site already, when you’d like to start and a rough budget — the project form asks just that. I come back with questions or a quote. Once we agree the scope, the price is fixed.
