I design and build websites, and design interfaces for digital products — for startups, founders and product teams, working on my own as Prokhorov®.

(01)About Me

I care about clear structure, and about websites and interfaces that still hold together as they grow.

I started as a UI/UX designer on Kwork in 2022, and since 2023 I’ve worked independently, as Prokhorov® — with international clients and startups, from research and prototyping to the final interface and launch.

In 2026 I joined Zeroqode to work on Playgram.ai, a collaborative AI workspace: the product interface, the landing pages, and close work with the developers throughout.

I studied advertising and PR at the Kyrgyz‑Russian Slavic University. I work in English and Russian.

(03)How I Work

It’s just me. You talk directly to the person doing the work — no account managers, no hand‑offs between people.

I start with the business, the audience and the goals, turn that into a clear structure, flow and content hierarchy, and only then design how it looks. After that I build it, test it and put it live.

I build the websites I design — this one included. For product interfaces I work alongside your development team through handoff.

I’m based in Bishkek, Kyrgyzstan (UTC+6) and work remotely and async with founders and teams worldwide.
