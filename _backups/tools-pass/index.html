(02)Info

I work with startups, founders and product teams — on their websites, from structure to launch, and on interfaces for SaaS and AI products.

I start with structure and content, then design the site and build it myself in Framer or Webflow — so the design you approve is the site that goes live. For product interfaces, I deliver UI and a design system your developers can build from.

I’ve been doing client work for more than four years, independently since 2023. I studied advertising and PR at the Kyrgyz‑Russian Slavic University and work in English and Russian.

(07)Questions

(04)
Do you work remotely?

Yes. I’m based in Bishkek, Kyrgyzstan (UTC+6) and work remotely and async with founders and teams worldwide.

Is it just you, or a team?

Just me. The person who designs your site is the person who builds it — no account managers, no hand‑offs between people.

Do you build what you design?

Yes, for websites — I build them in Framer and Webflow. For product interfaces I design the UI and work alongside your development team through handoff.

Can we start with an audit?

Yes. A UX audit reviews usability, structure, technical SEO and performance, and ends with a prioritised list of changes — which I can then make myself.
