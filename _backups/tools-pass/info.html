(01)About Me

I studied advertising and PR at the Kyrgyz‑Russian Slavic University. I work in English and Russian.

(04)How I Work

It’s just me. You talk directly to the person doing the work — no account managers, no hand‑offs between people.

I start with the business, the audience and the goals, turn that into a clear structure, flow and content hierarchy, and only then design how it looks. After that I build it, test it and put it live.

I design and build websites in Framer and Webflow — this one I built by hand. For product interfaces I work alongside your development team through handoff.

I’m based in Bishkek, Kyrgyzstan (UTC+6) and work remotely and async with founders and teams worldwide.
