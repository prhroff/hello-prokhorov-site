(07)Questions

(06)
Is it just you, or a team?

Just me. The person you talk to is the person who plans, designs and builds your site — no account managers, and nothing lost between a designer and a developer.

Do you build what you design?

Yes, for websites. I build them myself — in code, like this one, or in Framer, whichever fits the project — so the design you approve is the site that goes live. For SaaS and AI products, your engineers build the app: I design the interface and a design system, and work alongside them through handoff.

We already have a website. Do we have to start over?

Not necessarily. If the site mostly works, I can start by reviewing its usability, structure, technical SEO and performance, give you a prioritised list of changes and make them myself. If it needs replacing, the redesign starts from the brand and content you already have. And if you have a finished design, I can simply build it.

Can we update the site ourselves after launch?

Yes. When you want to edit the site yourselves, I build it in Framer: you change text and images in the browser and add blog posts, case studies or other repeating content through its CMS — without touching code or the layout. I set the CMS up around the content you’ll actually change.

Do you work remotely?

Yes, with clients worldwide. I’m based in Bishkek, Kyrgyzstan (UTC+6) and work remotely and async, so the project doesn’t depend on sharing a time zone. I work in English and Russian.

How does a project start?

With a short message: what you’re building, whether there’s a site already, when you’d like to start and a rough budget — the project form asks just that. I come back with questions or a quote. Once we agree the scope, the price is fixed and the work begins with your business, audience and goals.
